## Brand & Style
Vancouver+ Dark is an ultra-refined, telemetry-driven developer and analytics design system engineered for high-density cognitive throughput. Blending technical minimalism with chromatic cyber-tactile accents, it couples deep obsidian root layers with vibrant spectral accents—electric periwinkle, sunset rose, and warm amber.

The interface prioritizes sub-second comprehension and cognitive calmness during long telemetry sessions. It achieves depth through tonal surface stratification, glowing specular indicators, and crisp monospaced telemetry telemetry readouts without visual friction or distracting heavy lines.

## Colors
The palette is built on an absolute obsidian root foundation (`#0D0E14`) stepping progressively up through low-reflection surface tiers (`#121319`, `#1E1F25`, `#292A30`). 

- **Primary (`#7C77F4`):** Electric Periwinkle anchors system brand identification, active navigation states, and primary focal controls.
- **Secondary (`#F43F85`):** Radiant Sunset Rose acts as the apex accent in circular gauges, high-alert telemetry indicators, and performance peaks.
- **Tertiary (`#F7D070`):** Golden Honey Amber serves as the origin anchor for radial metric curves and velocity indicators.
- **KPI Metrics:** Four distinct high-chroma channels isolate telemetry streams: Sales Amber (`#F59E0B`), Orders Violet (`#8B5CF6`), Product Rose (`#EC4899`), and Network Cyan (`#06B6D4`).

All color pairings maintain WCAG AAA compliance against their respective dark container strata.

## Typography
The system enforces a dual-type engine:
1. **Plus Jakarta Sans** governs structural UI chrome, navigation, headers, and descriptive body copy with geometric friendliness and optical clarity.
2. **JetBrains Mono** is reserved strictly for tabular numbers, hex color codes, telemetry rates, system statuses, and metric card indicators.

Letter spacing is intentionally pulled tighter (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on display and metric figures to compress data density, while monospace labels expand with `+0.04em` tracking for legibility at micro scales.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column grid anchors the central content canvas with an outer padding margin of `2rem` (32px) on desktop, condensing to `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports. Columns are separated by consistent `1.25rem` (20px) gutters.

Fixed layout shells maintain a persistent `16rem` (256px) sidebar on the left and a floating `4rem` (64px) glassmorphic utility header along the top. Spacing within atomic cards follows an 8pt rhythmic cadence: `0.5rem` (8px) for tightly grouped badges and metadata, `1rem` (16px) for module body padding, and `1.5rem` (24px) for major card and container insets.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed via non-destructive tonal stratification rather than stark border outlines:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#0D0E14` absorbs ambient light and forms the background backdrop.
- **Level 0.5 (Workspace Dim):** `#121319` establishes fixed structural framing (e.g., sidebars and application shell boundaries).
- **Level 1 (Card Container):** `#1E1F25` lifts analytical cards and modules above the canvas with a soft `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)` drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Strata):** `#292A30` hosts elevated flyouts, popovers, and interactive modals backed by specular diffuse glows (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(134,129,255,0.35)`).
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation headers employ an 80% opacity mask with `backdrop-blur-xl` to blend incoming telemetry stream data smoothly underneath.

## Shapes
Interactive primitives and card containers rely on a smooth 16px (`1rem` to `1.5rem` / Level 2 roundedness) border curve, balancing technical precision with modern organic softness. Inner component wells, inputs, and list row items nest smoothly with `12px` (`0.75rem`) radii, while categorical tags, operational pills, and interactive toggles use fully rounded `9999px` capsules.

## Components

- **Buttons & Pills:** Primary triggers use `#8681FF` container fills with dark indigo text (`#1A0091`) paired with saturated periwinkle drop glows. Secondary buttons rely on elevated surface containers (`#292A30`) with `#E3E1EA` text. Interactive pills transition with smooth background and text luminance shifts.
- **Metric Cards & Strata Tiles:** Built with `#1E1F25` backgrounds, featuring a top-row monospace label, centered high-contrast numeric readout in JetBrains Mono, and bottom trend indicators with directional colored micro-deltas.
- **Inputs & Search Loupes:** Contained within `#0D0E14` inset wells with 12px border radii, subtle focus rings in primary periwinkle (`#7C77F4`), and ghost text in `#918F9F`.
- **Telemetry Gauges & Progress Bars:** Semi-circular 180° SVG arcs use continuous dual-stop linear gradients transitioning from Golden Honey (`#F7D070`) to Sunset Rose (`#F43F85`) over dark `#292A30` tracks. Linear product popularity meters utilize fully rounded 8px track heights with solid accent fillbars.
- **Status Badges & Chips:** Compact capsules with monospace uppercase text, muted background pills (`primary/10`, `surface-container-high`), and pulsing 6px colored telemetry pips.
